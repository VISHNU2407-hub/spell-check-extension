Privacy notice

Enabling AI correction sends eligible typed text only (never passwords, payment fields, or full pages) to https://api.groq.com for correction. Keys are stored in chrome.storage.local on your device — client-side keys cannot be fully protected. For publicly distributed builds, use a secure backend proxy instead of a shared key.
